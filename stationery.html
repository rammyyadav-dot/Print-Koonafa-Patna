Choose with confidence

The right format makes a difference.

Choose stationery for how it will be used, not just how it looks. Ask about the paper’s feel, thickness and compatibility before a large order.

For letters & office documents
A4 letterheads give you room for everyday correspondence. If you will overprint them in an office printer, confirm the paper and ink compatibility first.
For sending A4 documents
Choose C4 for unfolded sheets, C5 for A4 folded once, or DL for A4 folded into thirds. Allow for the thickness of the contents.
For notes & prescriptions
A5 keeps the format compact; A4 gives more writing space. Specify the sheet count per pad and the total number of pads.
For duplicate records
A 2-part NCR set gives an original and one copy. A 3-part set gives an original and two copies. Check numbering and form fields carefully.

Before you send your files

A better print starts with better artwork.

Have a finished design? Send it with your requirements. Need layout help? Share your logo and text, and ask the team about artwork support and any associated charges.

  • Send a print-ready PDF where possible. Include your logo separately if you have a vector version.
  • Use sharp images and clean typography. Aim for 300 dpi at print size; embed fonts or convert them to outlines.
  • Confirm the setup for your product. Ask about CMYK colour, bleed and safe margins rather than assuming one template fits every job.
  • Check every detail in the artwork. Review spelling, phone numbers, addresses, GSTIN, doctor details, numbering and page order before production.

What is the minimum order for stationery printing?

Minimum quantities vary by product. Listed options include 500 letterhead sheets, 100 envelopes, one 50-sheet prescription pad and 10 corporate diaries. Tell us the quantity you need so the team can confirm the available batch size.

Which envelope size fits an A4 document?

C4 envelopes hold A4 sheets without folding. C5 envelopes hold A4 folded once, while DL envelopes typically hold A4 folded into thirds. Confirm the usable internal size and document thickness before choosing.

Can you print a doctor’s prescription pad?

Yes. The listed prescription pads are available in A4 or A5 with 50 sheets per pad. Supply the doctor’s name, qualifications, registration number, clinic logo, address and contact details, and check their accuracy in the artwork.

What artwork should I send for printing?

Send a print-ready PDF where possible, along with your logo and the required size. Use high-resolution images, embed fonts or convert them to outlines, and ask the team to confirm bleed, colour setup and safe margins for the chosen product.

Can I get a GST invoice for a business order?

GST invoicing is available for B2B orders. Share your legal business name, billing address and GSTIN when requesting the quote. Confirm the applicable tax, invoice details and delivery charges before payment.

Is same-day stationery printing available?

Turnaround depends on the product, quantity, paper, finishing and artwork readiness. Share your required date before ordering and ask the team to confirm production and dispatch timing for your specific job. Same-day completion is not assumed for every product.
